Simulate a hub-and-spoke relay

Parcels travel hub to hub; no truck leg is longer than 500 road km (driver hours limit). For each leg a test truck based at the leg's start hub is loaded by scan, drives the real road route, pays official NHAI tolls, is unloaded at the next hub and drives back empty. Parcels are sorted at each hub before the next leg. The whole relay always replays in 5 minutes; costs come only from the simulated positions and scans, so the replay speed never changes them.
